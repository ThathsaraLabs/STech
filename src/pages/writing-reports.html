Original Draft (Passive, Wordy, Ambiguous Terminology)

It was observed by us that when the temperature was increased by the experimenters to 85°C, a really big change occurred in the reaction rate, which kind of proved our initial idea that heat makes it faster.

Issues: Passive voice construction, informal qualifiers ("kind of", "really big"), lack of quantitative metric precision.

Elevating the temperature to 85°C accelerated the reaction velocity by 3.4-fold, confirming the thermal activation hypothesis.

Refinements: Active scientific phrasing, concise sentence architecture, explicit quantitative metric reporting.
