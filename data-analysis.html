Our 5-Step Data Workflow

A systematic methodology for statistical integrity and reproducibility.

STEP 01

Understand Question

Align statistical tests with research hypotheses and dependent variables.

STEP 02

Review Dataset

Audit missing values, normality, outliers, and data types.

STEP 03

Select Methods

Choose parametric vs. non-parametric tests or ML models.

STEP 04

Analyze & Validate

Execute code in R/Python and verify model assumptions.

STEP 05

Explain Findings
